# Short Answers

## 1. Difference between Google Knowledge Graph and Google Knowledge Panel

The Google Knowledge Graph is a database that stores structured information about entities (people, places, organizations) and their relationships.
The Google Knowledge Panel is the visual display of that information in search results.

---

## 2. How Google determines entity identity

Google determines entity identity by analyzing structured data (schema markup), consistent brand signals across the web, authoritative sources (e.g trusted directories), and linking relationships between entities.

---

## 3. When to create Custom Post Types instead of pages

Custom Post Types should be used when contents are dynamic, structured, and needs its own organization (e.g. products, events, listings, properties, reviews etc). Pages are better used for static, one-off content like Home, About or Contact pages.

---

## 4. Recommended plugins for speed optimization and why

* WP Rocket: Improves performance through caching, lazy loading, and file optimization
* LiteSpeed Cache: Server-level caching with advanced optimization features
* Autoptimize: Minifies and combines CSS, JS, and HTML
* Smush: Optimizes and compresses images for faster load times
